How to Use the WEBER Traveler Gas Grill

The grill is already unfolded and set up. Use outdoors only.

Gas Canister Location

The gas canisters are stored in a designated spot indoors. Please bring one outside only when using the grill, and put it back afterwards.

Fuel & Safety

  • Fuel: EN417-compatible screw-type gas canister (approx. 470 g).
  • Keep all flammable items at least 60 cm away from the grill.
  • Use only in a well-ventilated outdoor area, and do not move the grill while it is lit.

1. Preparation (Connecting the Gas)

  1. Check that the control knob is at “OFF” and the regulator valve is closed.
  2. Screw the gas canister onto the regulator by hand (do not use tools).
  3. Open the regulator valve and apply soapy water to the connection to check for leaks (bubbles).

If you see bubbles, gas is leaking. Close the valve, reconnect and test again. If it still leaks, do not use the grill.

2. Preheating

  1. Open the lid and light the burner (see next steps).
  2. Close the lid and preheat on “High” for 10–15 minutes (aim for about 260 °C on the thermometer).
  3. After preheating, quickly brush the grate with a stainless steel grill brush.

3. Ignition

  1. Open the lid.
  2. Confirm the control knob is at “OFF”.
  3. Open the regulator valve.
  4. Press the knob down and turn it counterclockwise to “Start/High”.
  5. Press the red ignition button several times until you hear the clicking sound.
  6. If there is no flame within 5 seconds, turn the knob to OFF, wait 5 minutes to ventilate, then try again.

If ignition repeatedly fails, refer to the manual for match-lighting or troubleshooting, and stop use if you are unsure.

4. Shutting Down & After Use

  1. Turn the control knob to “OFF”.
  2. Close the regulator valve.
  3. When completely cool, check the oil level in the drip pan and clean/replace as needed.
  4. For long-term storage or transport, remove the gas canister and keep it outdoors.

Cleaning

After use, heat the grill briefly and brush the cooking grate, then check the drip pan and dispose of excess grease.

Area Guide – Naruto Sightseeing & Dining

🚗 1. Main Sightseeing Spots

  • Naruto Whirlpools (Ōnaruto Bridge) – about 10 min by car. One of the world’s largest whirlpools. Sightseeing boats are very popular.
  • Ōtsuka Museum of Art – about 15 min. Famous ceramic tile reproductions of masterpieces; a world-class all-weather museum.
  • Naruto Park & Eddy Tourist Center – about 10 min. Great views from the observatory and a variety of local souvenirs.
  • Uchinoumi General Park – about 8 min. Large lawn and playground, ideal for families.
  • Roadside Station Uzushio – about 20 min. Stunning views and local gourmet “onion burgers”.
  • Awa Odori Kaikan (Tokushima City) – about 40 min. You can enjoy Awa Dance performances all year round.
  • Asutamu Land Tokushima – about 45 min. Science-themed park, great for children.
  • Iya Kazura Bridge – about 1 h 50 min. Traditional vine suspension bridge with thrilling views.
  • Roadside Station Kurukuru Naruto – about 12 min. Local produce, specialties and souvenirs.
  • Aisai Market (Aizumi Town) – about 35 min. One of the largest farmers’ markets in Tokushima.
  • Janohire Outdoor Resort (Awaji Island) – about 50 min. Fishing, dolphin encounters and more.

🍜 2. Recommended Restaurants

  • Binbi-ya – about 10 min. Famous for Naruto seafood rice bowls; often a long line.
  • Uzunooka Restaurant – about 20 min. Ocean view dining with popular Awaji onion burgers.
  • Naruto Wholesale Market (Market Restaurant) – about 8 min. Great for fresh seafood and breakfast.
  • Café Apollo – about 7 min. Retro café known for handmade scones.
  • Unatetsu – about 10 min. Well-regarded eel restaurant in Naruto.
  • Dōnoura Naruto Main Store – about 10 min. Popular sea bream ramen; visited by celebrities.
  • Arashi – about 12 min. Local favorite with many set meals and rice bowl dishes.
  • Shanghai Cuisine Fufurou – about 10 min. Authentic Chinese restaurant with a strong local reputation.
  • Sushi Katsu – about 10 min. One of the best sushi restaurants in Naruto (reservation recommended).
  • Sanpachi Ramen – chain with several branches; classic Tokushima ramen.
  • Ristorante Fishbone – about 15 min. Italian restaurant overlooking the sea, perfect for special occasions or lunch.

🚙 3. Transportation

  • Rental cars are available around Naruto Station and Tokushima Airport (Toyota, Nissan, Niconico, etc.).
  • Taxi: Naruto Daiichi Taxi – 088-685-5555

🛍️ 4. Souvenirs

  • Naruto Kintoki sweet potato sweets (e.g., Kurio Shoten)
  • Naruto wakame seaweed
  • Ōtani-yaki pottery (you can also try pottery-making)
  • Naruto sea bream and sudachi citrus products
  • Kotora-ya confectionery (popular sweets using Naruto Kintoki and wakame)

🌸 5. Seasonal Events

  • Spring (Mar–May) – Best season to see powerful Naruto whirlpools.
  • Summer (Jul–Aug) – Naruto Summer Fireworks over Uchinoumi.
  • Autumn (Sep–Nov) – Naruto Kintoki sweet potato harvest experiences.
  • Winter (Dec–Feb) – Illuminations around Ōnaruto Bridge and “Uzu no Michi”.

💬 6. Message from the Host

Naruto is a town full of attractions: nature, art and great food. We hope you will relax, take your time, and discover your own favorite places during your stay.
